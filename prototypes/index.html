What rounds 1 to 4 decided, now in the screens

The look is Apple’s, on a design system of its own: the gallery shows every component, the glass and the motion.

Disks, uptime, alerts and domains (round 4)Each node’s disks: health, use, growth, scrubs, encryption, mirrors; prepare, adopt, erase, replace a failed disk, move an app’s data, encrypt the system disk; the disk analyzer behind your passkey. Uptime and incidents for a year. Alerts per admin and level, quiet hours, a morning digest, the maintenance window. Domains: only HomersLab’s own records, every domain equal, sign-in on the main one. Memos as a feedstock, with its guarded fallback. On Home, hide one of two same-purpose apps. No wallpaper choice; setup’s defaults and Admin’s motion stay.
The Store, and asking for apps (round 3)Admin’s catalog is now a Store: Discover with a featured app, collections that scroll sideways, the purposes and a search; an app’s page with Get, Open or Update; Updates and Requests beside Discover; the catalogs you trust in its account sheet. Get still opens the install plan, and one passkey at Install approves it. Members ask for apps on Home, from the same Store, read-only, or in their own words; the admin approves (which only opens the plan, with them in its audience) or says Not now, and they hear the answer on Home.
The glass is adjustable (round 3)One level drives every glass surface: Clear, Balanced (the default), Frosted, or Solid, with a fine slider between Clear and Frosted. Choose it in Admin’s Settings › Appearance, in Home’s person menu (each person, each browser), or in the prototype bar’s “Glass level” for review. It glides on a spring; the device’s reduced transparency always wins; words keep AA at every level, their ink growing stronger as the glass clears.
Admin dark first; light glass that is glass (round 3)Admin opens dark on every device, in Home’s dark look; Settings › Appearance offers Dark, Light or System, kept per browser. Light glass is now see-through, over a wallpaper with teal, sky and sage blooms, and content tints the bars as it scrolls under them; every word still meets AA against the pixels behind it.
Glass and motion (round 2)Today’s Apple glass on the bars, the floating sidebar and tab bar, sheets, menus, toasts and Home’s panels, with a specular rim and depth; Home sits on a calm wallpaper. Screens push and pop, an app’s icon flies to its page, Install grows into its progress, lists close their gaps. Reduced transparency turns glass solid; reduced motion leaves cross-fades only (try both: State).
A lean first-time setup (round 2)One question per screen with the default chosen (the sign-in from the machine’s memory, NetBird, the domain from your mail address, its DNS provider from its name servers); the rest behind More options; progress dots; a token’s “Where do I find this?”; an illustration per screen.
An app store (round 2)Admin names apps by product. The catalog groups them by purpose; a household may run several for one purpose (Jellyfin and Plex here), and Home shows both.
An admin’s Home (round 2)It says when something needs a look in Admin, such as a failed restore test. Replacing a mesh token and making a new recovery kit ask for the passkey.
The lookThe system font (SF; Inter elsewhere), Apple’s neutrals, inset grouped lists, widgets. Teal accent (Classic teal, from the logo kit); status in iOS green, orange and red, green for what works. The logo is the kit’s Harbour H.
The install plan is its own pageIts address carries the plan’s id (#/plans/p-…). Change anything and it is a new plan with a new address.
One passkey per planA single step-up at Install approves the whole plan, the extras listed in plain words above the button.
Risky changesDeleting data, nodes, roles, sign-in settings, anything public, extras, restoring an app, eject, trusting a catalog, a join code. Updates, audiences, Remove, revoking a device and inviting an adult or a kid only confirm.
HomeProduct names with their own icons; a kid’s own layout of big tiles; away from the mesh, “Needs the mesh” and How to connect.
Backups every hourRestore points: every hour for 2 days, every day for a month, then Sundays for 3 months.
First-time setup in two halvesThe one-time link up to “mesh connected, certificate issued”; then this device joins the mesh, the passkey is made at auth.ferreira.family, and the home-network listener closes at the first sign-in over the mesh.
DefaultsThe recovery kit is refused in a browser on the primary; a failed first health check undoes the whole install (try it: State, then “Fails its first health check”).

The prototype bar

The dark bar at the bottom of every screen is not part of the design.

StateWhat is in memory after each step (plans and their ids, passkey approvals, installs), and the household’s situation: its meshes, who is looking, on the mesh or away, how the next install ends.
ThemeFollows the system, or forces light or dark. Add ?bar=0 to any address to hide the bar.
Motion and transparencyIn State: “Reduced” shows what the system’s reduce-motion and reduce-transparency settings do (cross-fades only; solid surfaces). ?motion=reduce and ?transparency=reduce do the same.
VariantsWhere a screen still has an open question, the arrows (or ← →) switch between answers. Round 1 settled the plan and the kid’s Home, so none is open now.
