# App icons in the prototypes

Apps on Home and in Admin show their product names with their own icons, which each app definition will ship.
Until the catalog exists, these files stand in for them.

- **Source:** selfh.st/icons, <https://github.com/selfhst/icons>. Most were copied from classic's `site/apps/`;
  `mealie.svg`, `grocy.svg`, `vaultwarden.svg`, `open-webui.svg`, `plex.svg`, `photoprism.svg` and `calibre-web.svg` were fetched from
  the same collection (<https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/selfhst/icons/svg/>) on 2026-11-10.
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>. Unmodified, except `memos.png`, which is resized to 128 × 128.
- **Not from selfh.st:** `bills.svg` is drawn for the prototype: the Bill tracker is the fake household's own app,
  from Inês's catalog.

The apps' names and logos are their owners' trademarks. They appear only to show which app a tile opens; no
endorsement is implied.
